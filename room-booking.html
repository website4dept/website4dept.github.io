Book a room

Seminar halls and classrooms, for members of the department — faculty, research scholars, postdoctoral fellows, staff and students. Use the official e-mail address the department holds for you.

Loading the booking form…

How it works. Choose a free slot in the calendar and fill in the form. We e-mail you a six-digit code; type it in and the request is sent. Your faculty advisor and the department office are copied in, and you are e-mailed again in the same conversation once it is decided. Nothing is held until the request is approved.

Who approves it. Seminar halls are approved by the Head of the Department. Classrooms are confirmed by the department office. If your e-mail address is not recognised, please ask the department office to add it — see Contact Us.
