How to reach

Route maps

From Kharagpur Railway Station

About 5 km by road · 15–20 minutes

  1. Kharagpur Junction (KGP) is on the Howrah–Chennai and Howrah–Mumbai lines.
  2. Take an auto-rickshaw, taxi or app cab from outside the station to the IIT Kharagpur campus.
  3. Inside the campus, follow the map pin to the Department of Mathematics.
Open this route in Google Maps ↗

From Kolkata Airport (CCU)

About 140 km · 3–3½ hours by road

  1. By car or taxi: take NH16 (Kolkata–Kolaghat–Kharagpur) direct to the campus.
  2. By train: go from the airport to Howrah station (about 1 hour), take a train to Kharagpur (about 2 hours), then an auto-rickshaw or taxi to the campus.
