Message from the Head

Welcome to the Department of Mathematics

It is my pleasure to welcome you to the Department of Mathematics at IIT Kharagpur. Our department is home to a community of faculty, research scholars and students who share a commitment to rigorous mathematical thinking.

We engage with fundamental questions in pure mathematics while building strong connections with statistics, computation and the many areas of science and engineering where mathematics plays a central role.

Our academic programmes aim to give students a deep understanding of mathematical ideas together with the skills to apply them. Our research spans a wide range of areas, and we value collaboration within the institute and beyond.

I invite you to explore our website to learn more about our people, programmes, research and activities. We look forward to welcoming you to the department.

Prof. Adrijit Goswami Head, Department of Mathematics
