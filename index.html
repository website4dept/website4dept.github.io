About the Department

A broad mathematical community within India’s first IIT.

The Department of Mathematics at IIT Kharagpur supports teaching and research across pure mathematics, applied mathematics, statistics, computation and mathematical modelling.

Our faculty and students engage with foundational questions as well as problems arising in science, engineering and technology, building connections across disciplines.
