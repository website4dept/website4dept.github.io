Research Area 01

Pure Mathematics

The pure mathematics groups pursue fundamental questions in algebra, analysis, geometry and topology, with long-standing strength in commutative algebra, combinatorics and graph theory, topology and functional analysis.

AlgebraAnalysisTopologyCombinatorics & Number Theory

Algebra

Commutative, homological and Lie-theoretic structures.

Commutative Algebra

Ideals, graded rings and modules, Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity and homological invariants of commutative rings.

Homological & Homotopical Algebra

Structure, representations and cohomology of Lie and associative algebras.

Algebraic K-theory & Algebraic Geometry

Research Area 02

Applied Mathematics

Applied mathematics is the department's founding tradition, growing out of Prof. B. R. Seth's school of continuum mechanics. The group builds mathematical models and computational methods for problems from science and engineering.

Fluid DynamicsDifferential & Integral EquationsScientific ComputingOptimisation & Operations Research

Fluid Dynamics

Biofluid, microscale and environmental flows.

Biofluid Mechanics

Targeted drug delivery, magnetic drug targeting, Taylor dispersion in non-Newtonian flows and double-diffusive convection.

FacultyP V S N Murthy
Microfluidics & Microscale Transport

Electrokinetic and microscale transport phenomena and computational fluid dynamics.

FacultySomnath Bhattacharyya
Turbulent & Open-Channel Flow

Turbulent flow and sediment and solute transport in open and ice-covered channels.

FacultyKoeli Ghoshal
Linear Water Waves

Scattering and radiation of water waves by obstacles.

FacultyRupanwita Gayen
Porous Media & Boundary Integral Methods

Flow through anisotropic porous media, biphasic mixture theory and boundary integral methods.

FacultyG P Raja Sekhar

Research Area 04

Theoretical Computer Science

Building on the department's pioneering M.Tech. in Computer Science and Data Processing and Integrated M.Sc. in Mathematics and Computing, the group works on algorithms, complexity, computational geometry and cryptography. Cryptography research is carried out in the department's Security and Information Lab (SIL).

Algorithms & ComplexityCryptographyComputational Geometry & TopologyBlockchain & Secure Computation

Algorithms & Complexity

Design and analysis of algorithms and hardness results.

Graph Algorithms

Algorithms and complexity for problems such as max-cut and geodetic sets on graph classes.

FacultyBodhayan Roy
Parameterized Complexity

Fine-grained complexity of hard combinatorial problems.

FacultyBodhayan Roy

FacultyRatna Dutta

FacultySourav Mukhopadhyay
